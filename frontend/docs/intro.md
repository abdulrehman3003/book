# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

**From Digital Intelligence to Embodied Systems**

Welcome to the future of artificial intelligence—where intelligence meets physical embodiment.

## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI represents the convergence of artificial intelligence and robotics, creating systems that can:

- **Perceive** the physical world through sensors
- **Reason** about spatial relationships and physical constraints
- **Act** in the real world through actuators and motors

Unlike traditional AI that exists purely in the digital realm, Physical AI must understand physics, handle uncertainty, and operate safely in human environments.
